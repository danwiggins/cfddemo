## 0:00–0:12 — The premise

> This is Traceback. We took one real tube of blood, sequenced its cell-free DNA
> on a MinION, and computed two independent signals: fragment length and
> methylation-based cell origin.

Keep the hero and **Real / Recorded / Not built** strip visible.

## 0:12–0:38 — The AI moment

> The bioinformatics calculates the evidence. The AI has a different job: test
> whether our written interpretation is reproducible. Here the report says the
> chart uses aligned reference span, but the reproduction instructions say to
> subtract a fixed 45 bases. Those are not the same method.

Click **Show the mismatch**.

> The reviewer catches the contradiction, rewrites the claim, and cites both
> exact passages. The public site replays this validated assessment; live
> Bedrock review is available locally.

## 0:38–1:02 — Computed readouts

Click **Explore the results** or scroll to the fragment chart.

> The first chart is computed from accepted BAM records with one explicit
> denominator. It shows a nucleosomal ladder and no short-fragment cancer
> signal. The second pipeline extracts methylation, overlaps Loyfer markers,
> classifies fragments, and uses non-negative least squares to estimate cell
> origin.

## 1:02–1:15 — Close

> This is a research feasibility demo, not a diagnostic. Copy number is not
> built. The point is that every conclusion remains attached to its algorithm,
> source, denominator, and limitations.
