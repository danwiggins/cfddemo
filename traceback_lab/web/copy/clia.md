# What CLIA means for this demo

Sources checked: 2026-10-17; applicability still requires legal review.

## Short

**CLIA** (1988) is the federal law, run by CMS, not the FDA, that governs labs testing human samples to inform someone's health.

**Research boundary.** The exemption in 42 CFR 493.3(b)(2) depends on whether patient-specific results are reported for diagnosis, prevention, treatment, or health assessment. Keeping output inside the team does not by itself establish exemption.

**What needs review.** Before reporting any individual result, including a flag, range or next step, assess its intended use and the laboratory requirements.

## Full

**Research use and the reporting boundary**

**What CLIA is.** CLIA, the Clinical Laboratory Improvement Amendments of 1988, is the federal law that sets quality rules for any lab testing human samples to inform someone's health. It is run by CMS (Medicare/Medicaid), not the FDA ([CMS: Research Testing and CLIA](https://edit.cms.gov/Regulations-and-Guidance/Legislation/CLIA/Downloads/Research-Testing-and-CLIA.pdf)).

**Research boundary.** [42 CFR 493.3(b)(2)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2025-title42-vol5/pdf/CFR-2025-title42-vol5-part493.pdf) excludes research laboratories that do not report patient-specific findings for diagnosis, prevention, treatment, or individual health assessment. [CMS guidance](https://www.cms.gov/medicare/health-safety-standards/clinical-laboratory-improvement-amendments-clia/getting-started-clia) describes the research exception. Internal use alone is not a determination that this laboratory or workflow qualifies.

**What needs review.** Reporting individual results for health assessment can bring testing within CLIA. Review the purpose and reporting workflow before sharing a flag, range or next step with a participant or clinician.

**What it would take later.** Either our own CLIA-certified high-complexity lab, or a partner CLIA lab that runs and signs out the test. Some states add their own rules: New York requires separate state approval (to confirm with counsel). [[FLAG: LEGAL — verify]]

**Where the FDA fits.** [FDA’s current LDT page](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/in-vitro-diagnostics/laboratory-developed-tests) states that the 2024 final rule was vacated on 31 March 2025 and that a final rule issued on 19 September 2025 restored the prior regulatory text. This resolves the earlier date discrepancy; it does not determine all federal or state obligations for this product. [[FLAG: LEGAL — verify]]
